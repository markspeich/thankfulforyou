Five 1920x1080 Workspace Layouts

Each option keeps Design Text visible, gives the queue more width than the current layout, and reserves enough editor space to show the preview plus at least two line-control groups.

New Requirement Preview must remain visible on a standard 1920×1080 monitor during active editing.
Queue Goal The left queue grows from roughly 310 to 388 px into a more useful 595 to 670 px range.
Control Goal Two line cards should stay visible without pushing the preview out of view.
Recommendation Option B is the safest production-first direction with the least scanning friction.
What the screenshot changed: the current editor is losing height in three places at once: a tall header action row, a large top control slab with wide cards, and a full-width status band before the preview. The options below specifically shrink those vertical stacks so the preview can stay meaningfully visible on 1920×1080.

My read: if we want the lowest-risk production improvement, Option B is the best balance.
